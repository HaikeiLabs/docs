# Sign in to Kei

Sign in to the Kei web app with your work account. Choose the organization and workspace where you will connect an agent. If you do not have access yet, ask an organization owner to invite you.

You need an organization owner or admin to complete setup that creates a runtime installation or changes group access. A member can continue with steps that their workspace role allows.

## Check it worked

The web app opens to your organization and the intended workspace. Confirm the workspace name before continuing.

For Kei CLI sign-in errors, see [CLI sign-in troubleshooting](/kei/reference/cli#sign-in-troubleshooting).

Stuck? See [Troubleshoot setup](/kei/get-started/troubleshooting).
