# What can I do here?

Your next step depends on your role and what you are trying to do.

## I am an agent user

If you use Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, or another agent connected to Kei:

- **Learn about policies:** see [Policies, explained](/kei/policies/policies-explained).
- **Request access:** if a tool call or command is denied, see [Ask for access](/kei/policies/ask-for-access).
- **Understand a denial:** if you do not know why something was denied, see [Why was this denied?](/kei/policies/why-was-this-denied).
- **Connect your agent:** if your agent is not yet connected, follow [Connect your agent](/kei/get-started/connect-your-agent).

## I am an organization owner or admin

If you manage policies for your organization:

- **Create your first policy:** follow [Your first policy](/kei/policies/your-first-policy) to set up a basic allow or deny rule.
- **Manage groups and autogroups:** see [Groups & autogroups](/kei/policies/groups-and-autogroups).
- **Govern harness commands:** learn how to control shell commands, skills, and file paths in [Govern harness commands](/kei/policies/govern-harness-commands).
- **Grant time-bound access:** see [Time-bound access](/kei/policies/time-bound-access) for just-in-time access grants (console only).
- **Test before you sync:** validate your policies with `kei policy test` in [Test before you sync](/kei/policies/test-before-you-sync).

## I want to understand how Kei works

- [How Kei decides](/kei/reference/how-kei-decides) — policy ordering and precedence
- [Policy file syntax](/kei/reference/policy-file-syntax) — source and target pattern reference
- [Autogroups](/kei/reference/autogroups) — how autogroups resolve at runtime
- [How Kei governs coding agents](/kei/reference/how-it-works) — architecture overview
