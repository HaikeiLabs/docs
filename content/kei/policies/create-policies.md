# Create policies

A policy says who may do what, and whether it is allowed or denied. Kei does not seed policies — administrators create policies for their workspace using the console or the `kei` CLI.

![Policies page showing seven example policies for engineering, support, and finance with permit and deny effects](/assets/images/policy-docs/policy-list.png)

*Example workspace policies include both permits and denies.*
